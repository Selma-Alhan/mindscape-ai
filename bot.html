How to run it (for the student presenter)
  1. Share this screen. Click a grey question (or type your own) and let the bot answer.
  2. Ask everyone: "Which fact is fake? Write 1, 2 or 3 in the chat!" Give 60 seconds.
  3. Ask students from the country in the fact to explain. Then click Reveal the answers.
  4. Play the 6 rounds in order. After the last round (the twin cities), click Who are you really? for the surprise ending.
